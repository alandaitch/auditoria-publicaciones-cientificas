Where to look

Theorem 3.2 and Appendix Theorem D.3.

Evidence

  • Set d=2, p=1, h=id and weights w_n=6/(pi^2*n^2).
  • Points s_n=(4*n^2/(n^4+4),0,(n^4-4)/(n^4+4)) lie in S2 minus the north pole.
  • Their stereographic images are (n^2,0).
  • The probability measure sum w_n delta_s_n has finite spherical first moment.
  • Its projected first moment diverges, since every atom contributes 6/pi^2.
  • Almost every directional W1 distance to the south-pole Dirac is infinite.

Limits

  • The old antipodal-Dirac argument was wrong and is explicitly retracted.
  • The correct rotationally averaged antipodal distance is pi*sqrt(2), not infinity.
  • The replacement uses an infinite-support measure approaching the excluded pole.
  • Finite empirical data and bounded/capped implementations are not refuted.
