Where to look

Page 2first contribution. Section 3.1, pages 4–5, Theorem 1 and Eqs.5)–(6). Page 7, (14).

Evidence

At the diagonal, every atom of Theorem 1 goes [1 + cos ((w1 − w2) x)] / 2 ≤ 1. At zero goes exactly one. Any positive mixture meets k (x, x) ≤ k (0.0), independently of Q. The target has 0.1 and 1.1 at zero and one. Approaching both with an error less than 0.5 would require k (1.1) > 0.6 > k (0.0). It's impossible.

Limits

The proposed kernel remains a valid covariance. We do not evaluate its performance and the additional flexibility of latent coordinate training.
