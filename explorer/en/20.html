Underdamped Langevin MCMC with third order convergence

Journal of Machine Learning Research 27(126):1–63 · 2026-06

What the study claimed. Sampling error is controlled by initial discrepancy in positions.

Observation. The bound uses only the initial marginal W2 discrepancy in position, omitting initial dependence between position and velocity.

What could change. Initial position–velocity dependence can also determine later sampling error.
