Vector-Valued Gaussian Processes for Approximating Divergence- or Rotation-free Vector Fields

Journal of Machine Learning Research,27(74):1–36 · 2026-03

What the study claimed. The proposed space contains all divergence-free fields on the three-dimensional domain.

Observation. Simple connectivity in three dimensions does not ensure a global solenoidal extension. Theorem 22 relies on that identification.

What could change. The Gaussian representation can exclude fields permitted by the domain's geometry.
